### Political Parties and Party Systems

#### Parties: #partytypes
 - Cadre party
 - Mass party 
 - representative party 
 - integrative party (on concept of Russo's general will)
 - constitutional party 
 - revolutionary party
#### Systems:  #partysystems
- One party (china)
- two party (USA)
- multi party  (pakistan)
- dominant party system (Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) 54 years, Indian national congress 30 years)


#### ***Faction : a group within the party that disagrees with the party. called as faction or tendencies. Bitterness in factional rivalry is called as factionalism.*** #faction
