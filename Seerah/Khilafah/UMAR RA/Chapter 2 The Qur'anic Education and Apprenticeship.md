1. The Concept of Allah (Tawheed)

- **Allah's Perfect Oneness:** 'Umar believed that Allah is absolutely One, free from any partners, wife, or child, and possesses attributes of infinite perfection. #AllahsPerfection
    - **Qur'an 39:66** — _“Nay! But worship Allah [Alone and none else], and be among the grateful.”_
- **Sovereign Creator and Controller:** He believed that Allah alone created, controls, and sustains everything in existence.
    - **Qur'an 7:54** — _“Indeed, your Lord is Allah, Who created the heavens and the earth in Six Days, and then He rose over the Throne...”_ #SovereignityOfAllah
- **Source of All Blessings:** He recognized Allah as the ultimate origin of every single blessing, manifest or hidden.
    - **Qur'an 16:53** — _“And whatever of blessings and good things you have, it is from Allah...”_ #SourceOfGood
- **All-Encompassing Divine Knowledge:** He believed that Allah has complete knowledge of all things on earth and in the heavens, whether concealed or openly displayed. #AllKnowing
    - **Qur'an 10:61** — _“...And nothing is hidden from your Lord [so much as] the weight of an atom on the earth or in the heaven...”_
- **Angelic Recording of Deeds:** He believed that angels precisely watch and record every human word and deed in a book.
    - **Qur'an 50:18** — _“Not a word does he utter but there is a watcher by him ready [to record it].”_
- **Testing of Servants:** He believed that Allah tests His slaves with difficulties and desires to expose their true essence and show who will patiently submit to His decree. #TestingOfServants 
    - **Qur'an 67:2** — _“Who has created death and life that He may test you which of you is best in deed...”_
- **Divine Protection:** He believed that Allah actively supports, helps, and protects those who are righteous and seek His protection. #DivineProtection
    - **Qur'an 7:196** — _“Verily, my Wali [Protector, Supporter, and Helper] is Allah Who has revealed the Book [the Qur'an], and He protects [supports and helps] the righteous.”_

---

2. The Concept of the Universe

- **Created and Ordered Universe:** He understood the universe to be completely created, structured, and humbled in willing obedience to its Creator. #universe 
    - **Qur'an 41:9-12** — _“Say [O Muhammad], 'Do you verily disbelieve in Him Who created the earth in two Days?... Such is the Decree of Him, the All-Mighty, the All-Knower.”_

---

3. The Concept of Life

- **Worldly Life is Transient:** He viewed the worldly life as temporary, brief, and ultimately insignificant compared to the eternity of the Hereafter. #ValueOfWorld 
    - **Qur'an 10:24** — _“Verily, the likeness of [this] worldly life is as the water [rain] which We send down from the sky; so by it arises the intermingled produce of the earth... until when the earth is clad in its adornments... Our Command reaches it... and We make it like a clean-mown harvest...”_

---

4. The Concept of Paradise

- **Hidden, Eternal Reward:** He believed that Paradise is a place of everlasting, unimaginable joy prepared as a reward for those who worship Allah in fear and hope and spend in charity.
    - **Qur'an 32:16-17** — _“Their sides forsake their beds, to invoke their Lord in fear and hope... No person knows what is kept hidden for them of joy as a reward for what they used to do.”_

---

5. The Concept of Hell

- **An Active and Terrifying Reality:** 'Umar held an intense fear of Hell and the Day of Judgment, which served as a strict deterrent guarding his conduct.
    - **Qur'an 52:1-7** — _“By the Toor [mount]... Verily, the Torment of your Lord will surely come to pass.”_ _(Note: Hearing these verses once caused 'Umar to collapse in fear and fall ill for an entire month__)._

---

6. The Concept of the Divine Will and Decree (al-Qadr)

- **Absolute Predestination:** He had unwavering certainty that everything was created by Allah and written in a Clear Record before it ever occurred. #Qadr 
    - **Qur'an 35:44** — _“...Allah is not such that anything in the heavens or in the earth escapes Him. Verily, He is All-Knowing, All-Omnipotent.”_
    - **Qur'an 6:102** — _“Such is Allah, your Lord! La ilaha illa Huwa, the Creator of all things. So worship Him [Alone]...”_

---

7. The Concept of Man (Self)

- **Dual Origin of Creation:** He believed that man has a dual origin: created initially from clay (with a soul breathed in by Allah) and subsequently generated from a drop of semen.
    - **Qur'an 32:7-9** — _“Who made everything He has created good and He began the creation of man from clay. Then He made his offspring from semen...”_
- **Purpose of True Success:** He believed that human honor, dignity, and a "good life" are exclusively earned through belief in Allah and the performance of righteous deeds.
    - **Qur'an 16:97** — _“Whoever works righteousness whether male or female - while he [or she] is a true believer... verily, to him We will give a good life...”_

---

8. The Concept of the Struggle with Shaytan

- **Active Enemy of Humanity:** He recognized Shaytan as a plain, devious enemy who actively whispers, sows discord among believers, and attempts to lead humanity astray.
    - **Qur'an 17:53** — _“...[Because] Shaytan [Satan] verily, sows a state of conflict and disagreements among them. Surely, Shaytan is to man a plain enemy.”_
