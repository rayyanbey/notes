#### Conceptual Differentiation:
- For multiple beings to exist, there must be something that differentiates them. Since God’s attributes (like being all-powerful, all-knowing, and uncreated) must be the same in any true creator, there is no possible difference — therefore, there can only be one Creator.
- If there are two trees, they will differ in size, color etc, even if they are identical, on bare minimum placement and position will play a role in differentiating them.
- Now let us apply this to the Creator. Imagine that two creators exist, called creator X and creator Y, and that whatever is true of creator X is also true of creator Y. For instance, creator X is All-Powerful and All-Wise; so, creator Y is All-Powerful and All-Wise. How many creators are there in reality? Only one, due to the fact that there is nothing to differentiate between them.
- If someone is adamant in claiming that there can be two creators and they are different from each other, then I simply ask, *“How are they different?” If they attempt to answer the question, they enter the realm of arguing from ignorance, because they will have to make up evidence to justify their false conclusion.*
#### Argument from exclusion:
- This argument states that existence of multiple GODS is impossible because there can be only one will.
#### Occam's razor:
- Occam’s razor is a philosophical principle stating that _“plurality should not be posited without necessity”_ — meaning the simplest and most comprehensive explanation is usually the correct one.
- There is no evidence for multiple creators of the universe; therefore, the simplest and most rational explanation is that there is **only one Creator**.
- Believing in many creators introduces contradictions and unanswered questions: How can multiple eternal beings coexist? What if their wills conflict? How would they interact or share authority?

#### Argument from definition:
- If there were more than one creator or god, the universe would fall into **chaos and disorder** — reason and observation show this is not the case.
- _“Had there been within the heavens and Earth gods besides God, they both would have been ruined.”_ (21:22)
- Multiple rulers naturally oppose each other; hence, if multiple gods existed, the heavens and earth would lose order due to their disagreements.
- Critics argue that, like a car built by many workers, the universe could be created by multiple beings. but there is a difference between the human facilities and the Power of GOD, he is all knowing and all powerful and has his own will.
- *Two kings cannot rule one city harmoniously; either one dominates or both fail, leading to ruin. (IBN RUSHD)*

#### Argument from revelation:
- If God has revealed Himself to humanity, and that revelation can be proven to be from Him, then whatever it says about God — including His oneness — must be true.
- Two ways to find GOD:
- **Internally:** Through introspection and personal reflection.
- **Externally:** Through communication or revelation from outside the human mind.
- **Individual differences:** Humans vary in DNA, intellect, emotions, and experiences, so personal reflections lead to conflicting ideas about God (historically 3,700+ concepts of God).
- **Limits of reason:** Human reasoning can prove that a Creator exists but cannot define His nature. Trying to fully comprehend God through introspection is impossible — like “a mouse trying to conceptualize a galaxy.”
- Knowing that God exists is like hearing a knock on the door — you know _something_ is there, but you only know _who_ it is when the person speaks. Similarly, God must “speak” through revelation.
- **External Revelation — The Qur’an:**   Islam asserts that the **Qur’an** is the only text that meets the criteria of Divine revelation.

#### QURAN AS THE SOURCE:
**Criteria for a True Divine Revelation:**

1. **Rational consistency:** It must align with the logical understanding of God (eternal, independent, non-physical).
    
2. **Internal and external consistency:** No contradictions within the text or with observable reality.
    
3. **Signposts to the transcendent:** Evidence within the text showing it cannot be explained naturally.
- Since the Qur’an fulfills these criteria, it is a genuine Divine revelation.
- _Our God and your God is One, and we are Muslims in submission to Him.”_ (Qur’an 29:46)
