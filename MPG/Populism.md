### Populism 
  - According to earnesto laclau populism is not an ideology rather it's a strategy to mobilize the masses, it can align with left, right, center any spectrum.

### Constructs of Populism:
- Binzarization: creating a dichotomy of good and evil among the classes for their political gain. US vs THEM jose mourinhio idea.
- I AM THE PEOPLE, the promotion of sheep and shephard mentality among the Populus. 
- De humanization
- Sainthood
- Oversimplification of problems.
