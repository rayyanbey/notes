#### Inference to the best explanation 
- Most of our knowledge comes from the testimony of others rather than direct personal verification. Even beliefs we consider certain, such as who our parents are, depend largely on trusting information from people and records. The passage also highlights that humans often use reasoning to find the best explanation for available evidence. Just as doctors infer that a woman is in labor from signs like contractions, or a mother concludes her child ate biscuits from the crumbs around them, many of our beliefs are based on the most reasonable explanation of the facts rather than absolute proof.  #inferenceToTheBestExplaination
