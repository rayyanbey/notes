
### Colonialism 

***Social Darwinism is ==a pseudoscientific theory stating that human groups and races are subject to the same laws of natural selection as Charles Darwin observed in nature.***

### Historical Context: 1870-1914
 - the first globalization paved the ways of inter continental travel and access to other cultures and market
 - the second indutrial revolution is the era of techonological advancements and the countries investing in these developements were labelled as technologically superior

### Reason for expansion:
 - the economic reasons included ( new markets, raw materials, capital investments and to find new lands to cater the growing population in europe)
 - the political reasons included national prestige to recover the lost glory (france), indicating new birth and newly formed glory (germany and italy) and finally maintaining the prestige(Britain). moreover it also includes the rivalry among the nations such as france vs germany, scramble of africa, the suez canal, and other sea routes problem among nations. 
 - geostrategic reasons included strategic enclaves that protected the national interest on the foreign ground such as presence of british army in malta, gibraltar etc.
 - ideological reasons include social darwanism, evangelical idea, and white man's burden


#### Conflicts and Diplomacy
 - berlin conference (regulation for colonization)
 - fashoda incident (territorial dispute among french and britian force in africa)
 - scramble for africa (invasion of africa by western powers)
