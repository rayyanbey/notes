4. ***Christopher Hitchens***
- Christopher Hitchens, one of the "Four Horsemen" of New Atheism, was famous for his powerful way of speaking and debating. Unlike the other three, he wasn’t a philosopher or scientist — he was a political writer and journalist. In his book _God is Not Great: How Religion Poisons Everything_, he mostly criticizes religions from a social and political point of view, though he also talks about philosophy and science.
- Hitchens believed Islam is based on a mistaken book (the Qur’an), which he thought leads Muslims to behave in irrational or extreme ways. He gives an example where he mocks Muslims for not liking pigs, claiming some wanted characters like Piglet and Miss Piggy removed from children’s books and toys. He even mentions a statue of a wild boar in England being threatened with destruction by Muslim activists.
- He also spends a lot of time listing terrible events caused by religious beliefs. For example, in Nigeria, some Muslim leaders claimed the polio vaccine was a Western plot to harm Muslims, so people stopped taking it. As a result, polio came back and spread to other countries.
- He argued that Islam is dangerous and based on false beliefs. Like other New Atheists, he said religion has lost its purpose in modern times. Science, not religion, should guide us. He called for a “new enlightenment,” where people focus on human reason and progress instead of going back to old, harmful religious traditions.
