#### Titles of Abu Bakar RA
 - A person with multiple titles is considered an important person in arabic culture 
 - Al- Ateeq (freed one): based on the hadith narrated by Aisha RA that prophet SAW said to abu bakar RA that rejoice you are ateeq of Allah from hellfire, multiple other reasons are mentioned in the book pg 31  #Al-Ateeq
 - As- Siddique: this title was also given by the prophet SAW, as Anas RA narrated prophet SAW , Abu bakar RA, Uthman RA and Umar RA went to uhud and uhud started shaking, then prophet SAW said to uhud that remain firm there is a prophet, a truthful , and two martyrs on you. #As-Siddique , He also had an attitude that reflected if prophet SAW said then khalas no nothing else period.
 - As- Sahib: This title was given to abu bakar RA by ALLAH in Quran 9:40 (the incident of migration)
 - Al-ATQAA
 - AL-Awaah: merciful
