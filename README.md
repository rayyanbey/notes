# Islamic Studies & History Research Vault

A comprehensive Obsidian vault dedicated to Islamic history, theology, and contemporary geopolitics. This repository serves as both a personal research database and educational resource for studying Islamic civilization, governance, and intellectual traditions.

## 📚 Vault Overview

This vault is organized around three primary objectives:
1. **Islamic Historical Scholarship** - Systematic study of Islamic history from the Prophet Muhammad through modern times
2. **Theological Education** - Learning and teaching Islamic creed (Aqeedah) and philosophical defense of Islamic thought
3. **Content Creation** - Developing educational materials on Islamic history and politics for multimedia distribution

## 📁 Folder Structure

### **Seerah/** - Islamic History & Biography
The foundation of this vault, containing detailed studies of Islamic history:

- **Khilafah/ABU-BAKAR RA/** - Comprehensive 8-chapter study of the first Caliph (Radhiallahu Anhu)
  - Chapter 1: Lineage, family, and early life
  - Chapter 2: His Islam, preaching, and trials
  - Chapter 3: Migration to Medina
  - Chapter 4: Participation in battles
  - Chapter 5: Life in Medina
  - Chapter 6: Death of Prophet Muhammad
  - Chapter 7: Pledge of allegiance and governance
  - Chapter 8: Usama's Army expedition
  
- **MY NOTES/** - Summary notes and quick reference materials on Seerah

**Supporting Materials:**
- 6 lecture PDFs on Abu Bakr (Radhiallahu Anhu)
- Complete Seerah booklet reference

### **Atheism Course/** - Theological Apologetics
A structured course with 15 markdown files providing Islamic responses to atheist arguments:

- **Main Content:**
  - Atheism and Islam overview
  - Atheism Class 1 introduction

- **Divine Reality/** (13 focused essays covering):
  - God's existence and rational proofs
  - Qur'anic arguments for Allah's oneness
  - Divine mercy and compassion in Islam
  - Objective morality and God
  - Design vs. chance in the universe
  - Science and faith compatibility
  - Refutations of atheist claims
  - Why atheism is irrational and unnatural

**Purpose:** Educational resource for understanding Islamic theology and interfaith dialogue

### **Aqeedah/** - Islamic Creed Studies
Structured lessons on Islamic theology and belief:
- 6 comprehensive PowerPoint lessons (Aqeedah Lessons 1-6)
- Quiz and assessment materials
- Progressive learning structure for students

### **salahuddin/** - Salah ad-Deen al-Ayubi Research
Detailed historical study of Saladin and the Crusades period:

- **Chapter 1:** Historical Roots of the Crusades
- **Chapter 2:** Causes of Crusader Invasion

**Source Material:** "Salah Ad-Deen al-Ayubi [Vol 1]" by Dr. Ali Sallabi

**Purpose:** Foundation for video content creation on Islamic history
